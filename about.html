Nod Factor Productions & Media

Where Retro Soul Meets Future Sound

Nod Factor Productions & Media is a Birmingham, Alabama–based independent music, radio, and media company founded by veteran producer and artist DaNod Factor (Pernard / Nod Factor).

Rooted in over 40 years of music production experience, the company specializes in soulful R&B, gospel, jazz, funk, and contemporary sounds with a strong “grown folks” vibe. The mission is simple and clear: bring music back to music — creating powerful, purposeful work that moves people.

What Nod Factor Productions Does

Music Production & Artist Development

Full production, recording, and artist development for original tracks, soundtracks, and independent artists.

Nod Factor Radio

24/7 online radio station broadcasting Soul, Funk, R&B, Hip-Hop, Gospel, Jazz, and originals.

Video & Media

Music videos, commercials, jingles, short films, animated brand stories, and promotional content.

Custom Content Services

Fast-turnaround custom jingles, animated commercials, and branded storytelling packages.

Direct-to-Artist Sales

E-commerce platform for independent music sales and artist support.

Signature Projects

  • Ongoing artist roster spanning R&B, neo-soul, gospel, hip-hop, and more
  • Live choir recordings, commercials, and multimedia campaigns
