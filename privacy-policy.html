Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Nod Factor Productions ("we," "our," or "us") operates this website and the embedded Nod Factor Radio player powered by Radio.co. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit this site and listen to our radio stream, and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

Radio.co Streaming Service
The live radio stream on this site is delivered by Radio.co. When you listen to the stream, Radio.co may automatically collect technical data including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and the date and time of your request. This information is collected by Radio.co directly and is governed by the Radio.co Privacy Policy.

Radio Stream IP Address
When connecting to our radio stream, your IP address will be sent to our radio service provider in order for us to track listening trends and provide licensing bodies with royalty reports. Once our service provider receives your IP it is immediately anonymised, deleted and becomes untraceable. This data is never sold on or passed to other companies.

iOS Apps
The Nod Factor Radio iOS App does not collect any user data when installed or launched on your device.

Android Apps
The Nod Factor Radio Android App does not collect any user data during use. In order to provide audio control during Phone App use, the App will monitor the 'state' of the phone App (Idle, in call, call ended) if applicable on your device. At no point will the App be able to listen in or derive phone numbers or data.

The Android App also requires access to local storage. This is to store its configuration for faster launch times.

Usage Data
We may receive basic, anonymized analytics about how visitors use this site (such as page views and general geographic region) through our hosting provider, Netlify. Netlify's data practices are described in the Netlify Privacy Policy.

Optional Google Analytics
With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-39Q552JBKJ) to understand website visits, page views, session activity, approximate geographic region, and browser and device information. Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish visits and sessions. The Google Analytics tag is blocked until you select Allow analytics. Selecting Use essential only keeps this tag blocked. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our website tag configuration.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

Optional Google Analytics cookies, including _ga and property-specific _ga_ cookies, are used only after you allow analytics. Your browser\'s local storage remembers your analytics choice separately from the older cookie notice. Accepting that older notice does not authorize analytics. Use Cookie settings in the footer of any current website page to change your choice. Withdrawing permission stops this tag and removes its first-party Google Analytics cookies from this site. If local storage is unavailable, your choice applies to the current page and you may be asked again on your next visit. The Nod Factor Radio Android app also uses local storage to save its configuration for faster launch times.

3. Third-Party Services

This site embeds a live audio stream from Radio.co. When you load or interact with that player, you are also connecting directly to Radio.co's servers. We encourage you to review Radio.co's privacy policy at https://radio.co/privacy to understand how they handle your data.

This site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify processes server request logs as part of normal hosting operations. See https://www.netlify.com/privacy/ for details.

When you allow analytics, your browser connects to Google for Google Analytics. Google\'s practices are explained in its Privacy Policy and its information about data from partner websites. Your website analytics choice does not control technical processing by Netlify, Radio.co, or their separate services.

4. How We Use Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Technical and analytics information is used to operate and improve service delivery and, when you allow analytics, understand website usage.

5. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through the Booking page on this site.

8. Text Messaging (SMS)

Nod Factor Productions offers two independent, optional categories of messages for Nod Factor Radio. Simply providing a phone number, joining rewards, submitting a song, or accepting website terms does not authorize SMS messages.

How SMS Consent Is Collected

Our SMS Text Preferences form presents two separate, unchecked consent checkboxes. Where SMS enrollment is available in other forms, the same independent categories must be offered. You can choose marketing texts, informational texts, both, or neither:

  • Marketing and promotional messages: Optional recurring texts from Nod Factor Radio / Nod Factor Productions about station promotions, show and event announcements, merchandise, and offers.
  • Informational and song-submission messages: Optional non-promotional texts about your song-submission status and related service or participation updates.

Checking either box authorizes only that category. We do not infer marketing consent from informational consent, an existing relationship, submitting a form, or a phone number. SMS consent is not required to submit music, join a rewards program, make a purchase, or access other services. We retain records of an affirmative selection, the provided number, time and source of submission, and the consent language/version to document what you selected. A missing or unselected checkbox is not consent.

Text Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month for the Nod Factor Radio SMS program. Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile Information and Consent Are Not Sold

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in information with third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information may be processed by messaging and website service providers solely to operate the service and send authorized texts. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for marketing.

Unsubscribe and Help

Reply STOP to a text to opt out; a final confirmation may be sent. Reply HELP for assistance or contact us using our Booking page. Carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Terms & Conditions.
