Nod Factor Radio Text Messaging Program

Nod Factor Radio, operated by Nod Factor Productions and Media, sends station updates, show and event news, promotions, and updates for artists who submit music.

Opt-In and Consent

Currently, you may opt in by texting JOIN to the number displayed on the VIP Rewards page. The online VIP Rewards form collects your name and email address only and does not enroll you in text messages.

If a web form offers SMS enrollment in the future, it will use a separate text-message checkbox that is unchecked by default; you must select it yourself to consent to receive texts. Consent to receive text messages is not required to submit a form, join the online rewards program, or make a purchase.

Message Frequency and Rates

Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out and Help

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation message. For help, reply HELP or contact us through the Booking page on this site.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
